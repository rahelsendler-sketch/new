1. Gerak Lurus Berubah Beraturan (GLBB)

$$v_t = v_0 \pm g \cdot t$$
$$h = v_0 \cdot t \pm \frac{1}{2}g \cdot t^2$$
$$v_t^2 = v_0^2 \pm 2g \cdot h$$

*(Catatan Rahel: Kalau ayam dilempar ke atas, kecepatannya di puncak = 0 m/s!)*

2. Gerak Parabola (Lintasan Percikan Sambel)

$$X_{\text{maks}} = \frac{v_0^2 \cdot \sin(2\alpha)}{g}$$
$$Y_{\text{maks}} = \frac{v_0^2 \cdot \sin^2(\alpha)}{2g}$$

*(Niko nyoret: Sudut lemparan saus 45° paling pas kena jidat Noah! 🎯🤣)*
